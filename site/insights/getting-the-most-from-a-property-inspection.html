Getting the most from a property inspection

What to bring, what to look at and what to ask, so one visit gives you what you need to decide.

18 September 2026

An inspection is your best chance to find out what photos can’t tell you. A little preparation makes it count.

Before you go

Write down your must-haves and your questions. If you’re buying, ask for copies of the documents in advance so you can raise questions on site.

On the day

  • Run taps and flush toilets
  • Try switches and sockets
  • Open windows and doors
  • Look for damp, cracks and fresh paint over patches
  • Check phone signal in each room
  • Walk the street and look at the drainage

After the visit

Note what you liked and what worried you while it’s fresh, then send your follow-up questions in one message. It’s easier to get clear answers that way.
